## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an ultra-refined, discreet private-wealth intelligence interface. It rejects bright consumer-fintech cliches, opting instead for the quiet authority of legacy merchant banks, modern family offices, and bespoke portfolio stewards. The emotional tone is deliberate, impenetrable, and deeply prestigious—evoking discernment, financial sovereignty, and absolute clarity.

The visual language synthesizes High-End Minimalist Editorial and Subtle Tactile Glassmorphism. Deep obsidian and muted forest backdrops create a subterranean sanctuary where numbers and editorial insights emerge through illuminated warm ivory text and brushed champagne gold accoutrements. Layouts favor generous architectural breathing room, precise hairline dividing registers, and translucent surface stratification over overt ornamentation.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in low-luminance forest-obsidian tones, accented with brushed noble metals and natural mineral pigments:

- **Base Obsidian Canvas (`#0B1410`):** The master ambient backdrop, providing depth and removing visual noise.
- **Surface Tier 1 / Deep Emerald Charcoal (`#101C16`):** The primary container ground for macro modules, side navigation, and analytical cards.
- **Surface Tier 2 / Elevated Forest (`#15241C`):** High-priority overlays, modal sheets, and active focus surfaces.
- **Primary / Brushed Champagne Gold (`#C5A869`):** Reserved for sovereign signals: key performance markers, primary calls to execution, active state indicators, and investment badges.
- **Tertiary / Shimmer Gold (`#D8BF84`):** Interactive hover states, micro-sparkline highlights, and numeric accents.
- **Secondary / Muted Sage (`#8BA393`):** Editorial subheadings, portfolio growth trend lines, and contextual metadata.
- **Text Ivory Master (`#F4F6F2`):** Primary display and high-prominence figures.
- **Text Antique Ivory (`#EDE8DE`):** Body narrative, long-form financial analysis, and standard tabular entries.
- **Glass Accents & Ghost Borders:** Delicate translucent strokes composed of `rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.15)` for champagne focus highlights and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` for ambient division.

## Typography
Typography is paired to balance heritage authority and fiscal precision. 

- **Headlines and Editorial Prose (Newsreader):** Used for large balance declarations, portfolio thesis titles, AI advisory commentaries, and section headers. High-register displays utilize slight negative tracking with italic flourishes on key metrics to reinforce private-journal intimacy.
- **Interface, Functional Data, and Labels (Manrope):** Geometric and balanced, Manrope handles tabular figures, ledger line-items, chart axes, controls, and micro-labels. Small labels utilize uppercase styling with wide letter-spacing (`0.06em`–`0.08em`) to mirror premium horology and bullion engravings.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be enabled across all asset ledgers and performance readouts to maintain strict column alignment.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a disciplined 12-column grid for wide workspaces, collapsing cleanly to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

- **Canvas Anchoring:** Desktop structures maintain generous outer boundaries (`3rem`) to prevent dense financial readouts from touching screen edges. Content is grouped into architectural suites rather than cluttered tiles.
- **Rhythm & Density:** Standard interfaces rely on vertical modules divided by `space-lg` (`1.75rem`) to `space-xl` (`2.5rem`), giving critical net-worth intelligence ample stature. 
- **Ledger Spacing:** Fine tables and ledger rows utilize compact vertical padding (`space-sm`) paired with consistent horizontal gutters (`1.5rem`), ensuring scan-efficiency without sacrificing elegance.
- **Reflow Logic:** On viewports under `768px`, compound ledger cards stack into serialized accordion panels, shifting secondary analytical graphs off-canvas into full-bleed slide sheets.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids simulated heavy drop-shadows, instead applying layered glassmorphism, precise illumination edges, and tonal stacking:

- **Surface Tiers:** 
  - `Level 0 (Canvas)`: Flat `#0B1410`.
  - `Level 1 (Card/Container)`: Backdrop `#101C16` with a hairline stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - `Level 2 (Active/Floating Inspector)`: Backdrop `#15241C` supported by a frosted filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) and a hairline stroke of `rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.15)`.
- **Illumination & Shadow Profile:** When modals or floating command trays require elevation, use an ultra-diffused, ambient obsidian falloff: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(3, 7, 5, 0.75)`, paired with an interior top specular highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- **Hairline Registration:** Visual separation is driven by single-pixel rules rather than elevation drops, producing an architectural, blueprint-like fidelity.

## Shapes
The shape system adopts a low, tailored curvature (`roundedness: 1`). Corners maintain an architectural crispness:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Micro-chips):** `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Cards & Data Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Modal Dialogs & Command Sheets (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px).

Rounded pill shapes are restricted strictly to micro-status indicators (e.g., algorithmic asset classification tags) to preserve the sharp, bespoke character of the private banking environment.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary Sovereign:* Solid `#C5A869` background with `#0B1410` typography, set in Manrope SemiBold. No drop shadow; subtle brightness transition to `#D8BF84` on hover.
  - *Secondary / Ghost:* Transparent fill, bordered by `1px solid rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.3)` with `#EDE8DE` text. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.08)`.
  - *Quiet Obsidian:* Subtle fill of `#15241C` with `#8BA393` typography and a hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.

- **Input Fields & Search Trays:**
  - Background: `#101C16`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - Text: `#F4F6F2`, placeholder in `#8BA393` at 50% opacity.
  - Focus State: Border transitions to `rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.5)` accompanied by an inner hairline glow; default browser outlines are fully suppressed.

- **Cards & Ledger Modules:**
  - Constructed using `#101C16` backgrounds framed with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - Card headers feature Newsreader titles in `#F4F6F2` alongside tracking metrics styled in small uppercase Manrope (`#8BA393`).

- **Chips & Badges:**
  - Compact height (`24px`), `0.25rem` radius, colored with deep green backgrounds (`#15241C`) and framed by delicate champagne gold (`rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.25)`). Font size is strictly `0.6875rem` (`label-sm`).

- **Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios):**
  - Square or circular frames (`16px`) in obsidian `#101C16` with a border of `rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.4)`. Checked states reveal a solid `#C5A869` core with a dark charcoal glyph.

- **AI Synthesis Panel (Specialized Component):**
  - Frosted translucent card (`rgba(21, 36, 28, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) highlighted with an asymmetric gold accent border along the left spine (`2px solid #C5A869`). Body recommendations render in Newsreader italic (`body-lg`) to present generative advice with private-briefing dignity.
