## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet authority, intellectual rigor, and bespoke financial stewardship. It speaks to emerging high-earners, ambitious postgraduate scholars, and young founders who view wealth management not as a series of stressful chores or gamified micro-transactions, but as an artful, enduring discipline. The aesthetic balances classic private-banking tradition with modern computational intelligence.

The visual style merges **Editorial Luxury Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Frosted Glassmorphism**:
- Generous editorial margins, asymmetrical balance, and deliberate pacing mirror high-end financial quarterlies and private advisory monographs.
- Translucent veil layers, ultra-fine boundary lines, and brushed metallic flourishes replace crude drop shadows and loud alert banners.
- Data displays, balance tracking, and AI insights prioritize restraint, intellectual composure, and quiet confidence over urgency and cognitive noise.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from heritage library leather, private estate foliage, and refined precious metals, built over a radiant architectural base of warm ivory and calcified stone.

- **Primary (`#152A20`)**: Deep Forest Green. Forms the foundational anchor for prominent structural frames, master buttons, and primary visual anchors.
- **Secondary (`#C5A869`)**: Brushed Champagne Gold. Used exclusively for prestige signifiers, asset growth telemetry, selected states, and delicate ornamentation.
- **Tertiary (`#224A38`)**: Muted Emerald. Serves as interactive depth, elevated contextual backdrops, and affirmative financial metrics.
- **Neutral (`#181E1B`)**: Rich Charcoal Ink. The primary typographic medium, delivering optical legibility without the harsh artificial glare of pure black.

### Supporting Canvas & Structural Tones
- **Surface Canvas (`#FAF8F5`)**: Warm Italian ivory paper base.
- **Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure pearl-white with variable translucency for floating modules.
- **Structural Stone (`#EAE4D9`)**: Soft champagne-beige for card fills and divider baselines.
- **Muted Sandstone (`#D8CEBC`)**: Used for tactile inactive boundaries and quiet input tracks.
- **Sub-Forest Shade (`#0E1D16`)**: Deep obsidian-green reserved for immersive night views and high-tier account containers.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy pairs the heritage sophistication of **Newsreader** with the clean geometric ergonomics of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headings (Newsreader)**: Used for narrative insights, portfolio digests, AI advisory summaries, and high-impact net-worth statements. Rendered with natural optical sizing, medium italics for emphasis, and snug letter spacing.
- **Telemetry & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Used for all interactive UI, numerical ledgers, operational microcopy, labels, and forms.
- **Financial Figures & Currency Notation**: All Indian Rupee values (`₹`), percentage returns, and metric tickers must activate OpenType tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical column alignment across portfolios and statements.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy abandons cookie-cutter dashboard density in favor of an **Asymmetric Editorial Column System**. 

- **Grid Architecture**: A 12-column adaptive fluid grid on desktop (`margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`) shifts to a 4-column column system on mobile viewports (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`).
- **Asymmetric Composition**: Instead of identical tiled blocks, balance a wide 7-column analytical core with a bespoke 5-column editorial insight sidebar containing natural language advice, historical spending motifs, and wealth projection milestones.
- **Rhythm**: Element padding leans spacious (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) inside cards to let large serif typography breathe. Component internals utilize tight micro-spacers (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to group financial labels intimately with their corresponding telemetry.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical atmosphere, glass refraction, and diffuse warmth rather than heavy structural shadows.

- **Frosted Glassmorphic Strata**: Floating insight surfaces and navigation bars employ translucent white backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) backed by a multi-pass gaussian blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- **Hairline Bounding**: Surfaces feature a razor-sharp 1px border. On light ivory grounds, use `rgba(21, 42, 32, 0.08)`. Over glass overlays or saturated green tones, shift to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Amber-Tinted Ambient Diffusion**: Grounded elevation utilizes two non-directional, low-density ambient shadows:
  - *Standard Card*: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(24, 30, 27, 0.04), 0 2px 12px -2px rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.06)`
  - *Floating Dialog / Popover*: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(14, 29, 22, 0.08), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft, Tailored Silhouette** (Level `1`). Corners are gently eased to maintain an architectural, bespoke feel that evokes cut stationery and high-grade leather stationery rather than playful software widgets.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Checkboxes, badges, micro indicators, and tabular tags.
- **Card & Container Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px)**: Portfolio modules, financial metrics cards, input surfaces, and modals.
- **Display Structural Enclosures (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px)**: Hero editorial banners, persistent bottom docking bars, and full-screen wealth summaries.
- **Strict Prohibition**: Full pill buttons (`rounded-full`) are banned for primary controls, reserved strictly for micro metric badges and active status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Forest Green (`#152A20`) background, Pearl White (`#FFFFFF`) text, subtle 1px border of `rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.3)`. Border-radius is 8px. Hover shifts slightly toward Muted Emerald (`#224A38`) with an inner warm glow.
- **Secondary (Prestige)**: Frosted Sandstone backing (`rgba(234, 228, 217, 0.5)`), Rich Charcoal text, 1px hairline border of `#D8CEBC`.
- **Tertiary / Text**: Rich Charcoal with brushed gold underline active on hover.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Surface**: Pearl White (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border in Sandstone (`#D8CEBC`).
- **Focus State**: Hairline outline changes to Brushed Gold (`#C5A869`) accompanied by an ambient halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.15)`).
- **Numeric Fields**: Currency prefix (`₹`) fixed in Newsreader semi-bold, while user input renders in Plus Jakarta Sans with tabular alignment.

### Cards & Telemetry Pods
- **Ivory Folio Cards**: Multi-layered backgrounds composed of `#FFFFFF` layered over subtle `#FAF8F5`. No heavy borders; separation is defined by the 1px `rgba(21, 42, 32, 0.06)` outline and soft warm shadows.
- **Hero Statement Containers**: Deep Forest Green (`#152A20`) cards featuring hairline gold borders (`rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.4)`), ivory typography, and gold-hued telemetry tags.

### Chips & Financial Tickers
- Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.75rem`), font-size 11px uppercase with `0.06em` letter spacing. Positive investment metrics use muted emerald backgrounds (`rgba(34, 74, 56, 0.08)`) with tertiary text (`#224A38`), avoiding garish electric greens.

### AI Financial Insights Component
- Asymmetric card featuring an inset gold vertical accent bar (2px width, `#C5A869`), italicized Newsreader headline for generative advisory copy, followed by tabular granular actions rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans.
